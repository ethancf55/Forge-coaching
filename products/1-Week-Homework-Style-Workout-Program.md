# How to Use This Program

Treat this week like assigned homework. Complete each day’s task fully. Do not skip ahead or invent extra work.

### Rules
1. **Train on Days 1, 2, 4, and 6.** Days 3, 5, and 7 are active recovery or full rest.
2. **Warm up every training day** before the main work (3–5 minutes).
3. **Use clean form first.** If form breaks, stop the set or reduce load.
4. **Leave 1–2 reps in reserve** on most sets (hard, but not failed).
5. **Rest as written.** Rest is part of the program.
6. **Log your work.** Write loads/reps next to each exercise so next week is measurable.
7. **Miss a day?** Shift the remaining days forward. Do not double up two hard sessions.

### Effort guide
| Feeling | What to do |
|---|---|
| Easy / too light | Add small load or 1–2 reps next set |
| Challenging but clean | Perfect — stay here |
| Form collapsing | Reduce load or stop the set |

### Equipment (Main Program)
Dumbbells or kettlebells, a bench or sturdy chair, and floor space. If you only have bodyweight or full gym access, use **Variation A** or **Variation B** at the end of this document.

# Day-by-Day Breakdown

---

## Day 1 — Full Body Strength A

**Goal:** Full-body stimulus with simple compound patterns.  
**Time:** 45–60 minutes

### Warm-up (3–5 minutes)
- 60 seconds easy march or jump-rope style steps in place  
- 10 arm circles each direction  
- 8 bodyweight squats  
- 6 slow push-ups (knees OK)  
- 8 hip hinges (hands on hips, soft knees)

### Main workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat (DB/KB) | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Sit hips back; heels stay down |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Soft knees; feel hamstrings; don’t round low back |
| Push-Up (or knee push-up) | 3 | 6–12 | 75 sec | Body straight; full range |
| One-Arm Dumbbell Row | 3 | 8–10 / arm | 75 sec | Pull elbow back; pause 1 sec at top |
| Dumbbell Overhead Press (seated or standing) | 2 | 8–10 | 75 sec | Brace core; don’t lean back |
| Front Plank | 2 | 20–40 sec | 45 sec | Ribs down; glutes lightly tight |

### Cool-down (2–3 minutes)
- 30–45 sec hip flexor stretch each side  
- 30–45 sec chest doorway stretch  
- Easy nasal breathing, 5 slow breaths

### Coaching tip — Day 1
Your job today is **quality, not ego load**. Choose weights you can control for every rep. Write them down. That log is how you progress next week.

---

## Day 2 — Upper Push + Pull

**Goal:** Upper-body strength and muscle retention with balanced push/pull work.  
**Time:** 45–55 minutes

### Warm-up (3–5 minutes)
- 60 seconds easy shadow boxing or arm swings  
- 10 scapular push-ups (protract/retract)  
- 8 band pull-aparts or slow arm openers  
- 6 light dumbbell presses

### Main workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Bench Press (floor press OK) | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Lower with control; stop just above chest |
| Chest-Supported or Bent-Over DB Row | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Squeeze shoulder blades together |
| Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 3 | 8–10 | 75 sec | Stop if low back arches hard |
| Lat Pullover (DB) or Straight-Arm Pulldown | 2 | 10–12 | 60 sec | Keep elbows soft; feel lats |
| Lateral Raise | 2 | 12–15 | 45 sec | Light weight; no swinging |
| Dead Bug | 2 | 6–8 / side | 45 sec | Low back stays pressed down |

### Cool-down (2–3 minutes)
- 45 sec pec stretch each side  
- 30 sec upper trap stretch each side  
- Shake out arms; walk 1–2 minutes easy

### Coaching tip — Day 2
Pull as hard as you push. If rows feel weak compared to presses, keep the rest honest and own the top squeeze. Balanced upper work protects shoulders and posture.

---

## Day 3 — Active Recovery (Walk + Mobility)

**Goal:** Recover without losing momentum.  
**Time:** 20–35 minutes  
**Intensity:** Easy conversational pace

### Assignment
1. **Walk 15–25 minutes** outdoors or on treadmill (easy).  
2. **Mobility circuit × 2 rounds** (no strain):

| Move | Time / Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| World’s Greatest Stretch | 4 / side | Slow and controlled |
| Cat-Cow | 8–10 | Match breath to movement |
| 90/90 Hip Switches | 6 / side | Tall posture |
| Thoracic Open Books | 6 / side | Move from mid-back, not lower back |
| Easy hamstring stretch | 30 sec / side | Soft knee OK |

### Coaching tip — Day 3
This is still homework. Doing recovery well is how fat-loss training stays sustainable. Do **not** turn this into a hard workout.

---

## Day 4 — Lower Body Strength

**Goal:** Legs and glutes with controlled loading for recomp.  
**Time:** 45–60 minutes

### Warm-up (3–5 minutes)
- 60 seconds easy march  
- 10 bodyweight squats  
- 8 reverse lunges (bodyweight)  
- 8 glute bridges  
- 20-second calf raise hold

### Main workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Front Squat or Goblet Squat | 4 | 8–10 | 90–120 sec | Elbows high; upright torso |
| Romanian Deadlift (DB) | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Slow lower (2–3 sec) |
| Reverse Lunge | 3 | 8 / leg | 75–90 sec | Long step back; front knee tracks toes |
| Hip Thrust or Glute Bridge (weighted if possible) | 3 | 10–12 | 60–75 sec | Pause 1 sec at top |
| Standing Calf Raise | 2 | 12–15 | 45 sec | Full stretch at bottom |
| Side Plank | 2 | 15–30 sec / side | 45 sec | Hips stacked |

### Cool-down (2–3 minutes)
- 45 sec couch stretch or hip flexor stretch / side  
- 30–45 sec seated hamstring stretch / side  
- Easy walk 1–2 minutes

### Coaching tip — Day 4
Lower-body sessions burn energy and protect muscle during fat loss. Keep reps smooth. If lunges challenge balance, hold a wall lightly and own the range you control.

---

## Day 5 — Active Recovery (Easy Cardio + Stretch)

**Goal:** Light movement, better recovery, low stress.  
**Time:** 20–30 minutes

### Assignment
1. **Easy cardio 12–20 minutes:** walk, easy bike, or elliptical. Heart rate should stay comfortable.  
2. **Full-body stretch sequence (hold 30–40 sec each):**

| Stretch | Notes |
|---|---|
| Chest doorway | Elbows at ~90° |
| Lat stretch (overhead reach) | Side-bend gently |
| Figure-4 glute stretch | Supine or seated |
| Hip flexor stretch | Tuck pelvis slightly |
| Calf stretch on wall | Both straight and bent knee versions |

Optional: 5 minutes of easy foam rolling on quads, glutes, and upper back.

### Coaching tip — Day 5
If you feel wiped out, choose the shorter end of the cardio range. Recovery is not laziness — it is part of recomp.

---

## Day 6 — Full Body Strength B

**Goal:** Second full-body session with slightly different emphasis.  
**Time:** 45–60 minutes

### Warm-up (3–5 minutes)
- 60 seconds easy jog in place  
- 8 squat-to-stand  
- 6 inchworms  
- 8 light rows  
- 6 light presses

### Main workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Split Squat (rear foot elevated optional) | 3 | 8 / leg | 90 sec | Most weight on front leg |
| Dumbbell Hip Hinge / RDL | 3 | 8–10 | 90 sec | Same standard as Day 1–4 |
| Incline Push-Up or DB Floor Press | 3 | 8–12 | 75 sec | Choose the harder clean option |
| Supported DB Row | 3 | 8–10 / arm | 75 sec | No torso twisting |
| Dumbbell Curl | 2 | 10–12 | 45 sec | Control the lower |
| Overhead Triceps Extension or Diamond Push-Up | 2 | 10–12 | 45 sec | Elbows stay tucked as able |
| Hollow Hold or Dead Bug | 2 | 15–30 sec or 6/side | 45 sec | Quality bracing |

### Cool-down (2–3 minutes)
- Easy walk 2 minutes  
- 30–45 sec child’s pose or long stretch on floor  
- Note your best sets in your log

### Coaching tip — Day 6
Finish the week strong, not destroyed. Leave a little in the tank. Consistency across all 4 training days beats one heroic session.

---

## Day 7 — Full Rest (or Optional Easy Walk)

**Goal:** Reset for next week.  
**Time:** Optional 15–25 minute easy walk

### Assignment
- Prefer full rest if sleep, stress, or soreness is high.  
- If you want light movement: **easy walk only** — no structured training.  
- Prep next week: review your log, set training times, stock protein-forward meals.

### Coaching tip — Day 7
Recomp is a week-to-week process. Protect sleep tonight. Show up again next week with the same structure and slightly better numbers.

# Progression Notes

If you repeat this program for another week (recommended), progress in this order:

| Priority | How to progress |
|---|---|
| 1 | Add **2.5–5 lb** (or smallest available increment) when you hit the top of the rep range with clean form on all sets |
| 2 | Add **1–2 reps** per set before adding load |
| 3 | Improve **tempo** (2–3 sec lowering) if load options are limited |
| 4 | Add a **set** only after 2 consistent weeks of clean performance |

### When **not** to progress
- Joint pain (not muscle effort)  
- Sleep under ~6 hours for several nights  
- Form breaking before the target reps  

### Simple weekly checklist
- [ ] 4 training days completed  
- [ ] 2–3 recovery days completed  
- [ ] Protein hit most days  
- [ ] Sleep mostly protected  
- [ ] Loads/reps logged

1. Harder bodyweight variation (e.g., knee push-up → standard → feet elevated)  
2. Add backpack load  
3. Slow the lowering phase (3 seconds)  
4. Add reps within the range
